# Smart Recipe: offline recipes with a nutrient filter

Branch `offline-recipes` → `main`

## What changes
- **`data/recipes.json`**: the 24 supplied recipes, saved byte for byte. They replace the old built-in list of 10.
- **`data/recipes-more.json`**: recipes 25–111 from the 28 Oct table, as list-only entries (name, vegetables, time, 素/可素, tags). Their cards and detail pages say the steps and nutrient values are still to be added.
- **Smart Recipe tab**, on one screen:
  - 「我有這些蔬菜」 shows the 8 recipe vegetables first, with a 「顯示全部 40 款蔬菜」 button;
  - 「我想多攝取」 shows 5 nutrient chips;
  - 「只看素食」 is a switch;
  - the results update live, with a count in an `aria-live` region.
- **Filter**: one pure function, `filterRecipes`.
  - Recipes with every picked tag come first, then a 「部分符合」 group.
  - Results are sorted by picked vegetables, then matched tags, then shorter time.
  - When nothing matches, the page says which choice to remove.
- **Recipe detail**:
  - ingredients with amounts, and numbered steps;
  - the note, and the allergen line 「致敏物：…（請細閱醬料標籤）」;
  - a per-serving table with % of `meta.references`, then the tag rule and sources.
  - Vitamin C is hidden when its value is null.
  - Choy sum recipes show 「營養資料待核實」.
  - Recipes with tofu add a calcium note.
- **AI**:
  - It's now an outlined secondary button, 「用 AI 創作新食譜」, placed below the results.
  - It sends the picked nutrient ids. The server accepts only protein, vitA, vitC, calcium and fibre, and adds "favour ingredients that are good sources of: …" to the prompt. All existing rules stay.
  - AI recipes show 「AI 食譜未有營養分析」 and no tags or numbers.
  - The button is hidden while offline. If the AI fails, the offline list stays on screen.
- **Offline**: `sw.js` caches `index.html` and both data files (network first). It never caches `/api/`.
