How to finish this sign

  1. Host index.html and put the final URL in README.md ("Live URL").
  2. Make a QR code for that exact URL with any generator you trust, using error correction level M or higher. Save it as qr.png (at least 1000×1000 px) next to this file.
  3. In this file, replace the contents of <div class="qr"> with <img src="qr.png" alt="QR code">, and replace the text in <p class="url"> with the URL.
  4. Print on A5 at 100% scale. Test-scan the printed sign with an iPhone and an Android phone from about 1 m away before the event.
